How It Works

One paycheck.
A plan you both trust.

OurPlan helps you decide what each paycheck needs to do, then gives both partners the same plan, buckets, debt protection, and weekly meeting.

01

Enter income, split it into buckets, and add the cards and loans you're carrying — all by hand, no bank linking.

02

Fund each payday

Assign each paycheck to the plan. OurPlan protects your debt minimums first, then fills your buckets by priority.

03

Confirm together

Confirm what actually happened with Payday Promise, and invite your partner through encrypted CloudKit sync.

04

A guided 10-minute check-in to review what changed, make small adjustments, and start the next week calmer.
